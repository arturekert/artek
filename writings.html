Writings

A selection of occasional prose I would save from a fire, and some pieces written for the pleasure of explaining.

For the technical papers, see my Google Scholar profile.

  1. This Sentence Is False: A Small Collection of Puzzles Artur Ekert Festschrift contribution (PDF)

    Puzzles and meta-puzzles chewed over with Kwek across the years, mostly over dinner at one hawker stall or another. Solutions provided; no peeking.

  2. The Island That Sailed Away: How Madagascar Grew Its Own World Artur Ekert Illustrated essay

    An illustrated account of an island adrift — and of the singular world that evolved once it was on its own.

  3. The ultimate physical limits of privacy Artur Ekert and Renato Renner Nature 507, 443–447 (2014)

    A perspective on device-independent cryptography: privacy that can be guaranteed even with untrusted devices, given free choice.

  4. Beyond the Quantum Horizon David Deutsch and Artur Ekert Scientific American, September 2012

    An essay arguing that quantum theory expands, rather than limits, what can be known and computed; selected for The Best American Science and Nature Writing 2013.

  5. Less Reality, More Security Artur Ekert Physics World 22 (9), 28–32 (2009)

    How the violation of Bell inequalities makes cryptography secure even when the devices themselves cannot be trusted.

  6. Complex and Unpredictable Cardano Artur Ekert International Journal of Theoretical Physics 47, 2101 (2008)

    A historical essay on the Renaissance polymath whose complex numbers and games of chance underpin quantum amplitudes and probability.

  7. Cracking Codes, Part II Artur Ekert Plus Magazine, Issue 35 (2005)

    The sequel: public-key cryptography, Shor's algorithm and quantum key distribution for a general audience.

  8. Cracking Codes, Part I Artur Ekert Plus Magazine, Issue 34 (2005)

    A tour through the history of codes and code-breaking, from substitution ciphers to quantum cryptography.
